Build a new Contest is a mid long journey, so in that platform we offer
to your Organization a way to build it joining simple steps.
First is create and name your Contest designed, from your personal dashboard
reach the organization dashboard then click on Design New Contest.

**FOR CIRCUIT** we are in development to aim insert and manage circuit
contest. At now a field is present but for future use.

&nbsp;

![_](/docs/contest/add_img01.png)

&nbsp;

![_](/docs/contest/add_img02.png)

&nbsp;

Your contest is now an empty blu project sheet, fill the fields.  
![_](/docs/contest/add_img03.png)

&nbsp;

Field required are:

- Contest name,  
  english international version if differ from english
  for character and language
- pick a country, usually is presented the Organization country code
  but should be changed
- pick a Timezone, that help the platform to know
  when your contest open / close participation overall the world timezones
- Preferred internet lang code, for future use.
- Contest name in local lang, facultative
- Is A Circuit, for future use.
  The circuit, with all the contest in circuit,
  should be grouped under the circuit id code.
- Circuit Id, for future use.
- Contest Contact info  
  Space to insert info about Contest Secretary, address, email, telephone, in plain text
- Award Ceremony info  
  Where, When, Who... in plain text
- Fee payment info  
  In plain text it's a free partecipation? X for theme? X for Author?

After you fill the form and pushed the lower button you can,
even in a later moment, fill the remaing panels, each one simplest possible.
![_](/docs/contest/add_img04.png)

After inserted for contest Names and info, click on Calendar
