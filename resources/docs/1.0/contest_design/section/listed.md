## About

Almost every photography contest has at least one *theme* or *category*.  
At the very least, there’s an "Open" category, which basically means,  
*"Submit whatever you want, as long as it’s good."*  
In reality, having *categories*, *themes*, *sections*, helps the judges make two determinations: first, that the submitted work is eligible for the contest because it meets the requirements and corresponds to the theme and category in which it is entered; and second, which of the eligible entries is the best.  

&nbsp;

Furthermore, if the contest is patronized by a federation, the federation can assist the organizers and require them to adhere to certain standards, since the comparison of those images subsequently forms the basis for the compilation of rankings and statistics, the purpose of which is to award honors and artistic titles in photography.  

&nbsp;

The Theme n section can be helpful to made some automatical test over the submitted works,
when are required some weight and height max size, or works are monochrome qualified,
or other requirement that are usual for all contest. When you have "no limits"
simply use an higher limit.

## Reach Contest Section n theme Index page

To access the panel for adding a Contest Section Theme
—keeping in mind that we are currently dealing only with digital image contests—
navigate from your personal dashboard to the dashboard of the organization you belong to;
therefore, if a contest is already in the planning stages, you will be taken to the
contest planning panel.

&nbsp;

At the top of the page, the main navbar includes the “Sections” option.
Clicking on it takes you to the list of themes and sections already assigned
to the contest, where you can enter the details that define the contest section.
