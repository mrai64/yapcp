## And the winners are

Once the jury’s initial work has been completed, and the jury has
selected the entries to be admitted to the competition, the final meeting of the jury
proceeds, section by section, to review all the admitted photographs
and then, as a collective decision, the prizes are awarded.

&nbsp;

To this end, a panel listing the prizes awarded and
those to be awarded has been set up, along with a panel of works admitted to each section, from which
the works to be awarded a prize, a special mention
or other honours are selected.

&nbsp;

To access these two panels – which we recommend keeping
open simultaneously on one screen, or on two screens, side by side –
you need to navigate from your personal dashboard to the organisation’s dashboard,
which, under the ‘during the judging process’ section, leads to the
competition dashboard.

Next, how fill the prize board, one at time? Near the miniature view
you have a selector with the award code listed also in
prize Board. Select the right award for a work then
click on ASSIGN button. When all is ok and the prize
loaded, then refresh the prize board page.

&nbsp;

Note: when assigned, the award code disappear from available
values in the candidate page / list. No risk to assign twice
a prize. Remember: Award code are unique in contest, even
if more than a prize are "equivalent" or declared "the same",
i.e. Honorable Mentions, or Juror' Choices.
