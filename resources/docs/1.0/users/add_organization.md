## Add a new Organization

You see the organization list and YOUR society isn't in the list.
Add a New organization? You can. Even if you are a new register user in platform.

![01_from Organization List click on Add button](/docs/organizations/listed_img01.png)

Then click on Add a new Organization  
![02_empty_form](/docs/users/organizations/add_organization_img01.png)

Fill the few info required  
![03_fullfilled_form](/docs/users/organizations/add_organization_img02.png)

And that's all, a new Organization is in list  
![04_new_in_list](/docs/users/organizations/add_organization_img03.png)

And, (surprise?) in your dashboard, you are already an Organization member  
![05_you_r_member](/docs/users/organizations/add_organization_img04.png)
