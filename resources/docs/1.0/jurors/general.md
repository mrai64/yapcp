## Juror is

*A person who decides who has won a competition*

&nbsp;

**First of all, congratulations!**  
on being selected as a judge for one of the competitions hosted on our platform. The responsibility of viewing and selecting from - *what we hope will be* - a large number of images is a reflection of the honour bestowed upon you as *skilled photographers*, and/or masters of photography, and/or expert photo critics.

&nbsp;

Upon accepting the assignment, if you had not already registered on the platform, the organization has *created an account for you*, using your email address. In this case, because you were assigned a random 24-character sequence as your login password, the *first thing* you will need to do when you log in, for the first time, is to request a *password change*.

&nbsp;

You will receive an email from the yapcp platform containing a link, valid for a limited time, which will allow you to set a new password of your choice. Our advice to everyone, without exception, is to string together five short, random words that are easy to remember.

&nbsp;

Once you have updated your password, you can log in straight away using your login details – your email address and password – to access the platform, where you will be taken to your dashboard. Use of the platform is free of charge – almost always free. Fees and conditions apply only if a certain number of works are uploaded to the repository, or if they are retained for longer than a certain period (years). In any case, we invite you to read the [terms and conditions](/terms-of-service) and the information on the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy), available at respective links on the platform carefully, and the updated[User manual](/{{route}}/{{version}}/).  

&nbsp;

If you have any queries or questions regarding the use of the platform, or if the published user manual is insufficient, please submit one or more questions explaining your issue, accompanied by any information and/or screenshots you consider useful for understanding the problem, and send them by email to the admin group at gruppoadmin@yapcp.local
