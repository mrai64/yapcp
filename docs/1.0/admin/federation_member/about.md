## About Federation Member

On the platform, Federations are *Organisations that set the rules* for the organisation of photography competitions. Some Federations, as part of their rules, take on the responsibility of verifying that competitions are conducted properly, for example by requesting copies of RAW images for ‘Nature’ themes and sections.

In addition to this, information is provided to enable organisations to coordinate with the federations regarding various forms of patronage and sponsorship for competitions at national and international level.

Admins can therefore add users as ‘Federation Users’, granting them the ability to view and examine the image folders for the competitions, as well as the RAW images mentioned earlier.
