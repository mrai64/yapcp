## It's just a click

Once the jurors have finished voting for the section, the organizers can go to the competition dashboard and select the ‘ScoreBoard’ link.  

&nbsp;

In that page are listed the sum of vote, from better to lower.  
And you, as Organization, can define that all the works that have received a cumulative vote **⬆️ From X and up ⬆️** must be marked as "Admit to Contest".
This means that the jury, as a panel of jurors, considered those images to be more relevant to the theme and section of the competition than the others.

![_](./admit_assignment_img03.png.png)

&nbsp;

## Or a lot of click

An alternative to selection by vote is a group meeting in which a majority of jurors express their preference as to whether to proceed with or reject the images. This can also be done remotely, via an online conference, and creates a selection process that ultimately determines the set of works to be admitted. (Actually it's a *work-in-progress*)
