## It's NO 'just a click'

At the end of a competition or exhibition, producing a catalogue is essential. Sometimes this is the very reason why the competition was organised in the first place, as when printed in an elegant format, it becomes a publication that photography clubs use to promote their activities. The world’s leading competitions offer visitors an elegant and dynamic way of viewing the results and the entries that have been shortlisted and awarded prizes. Our aim is to provide you with an effective and efficient system for producing your competition catalogue, based on generally accepted standards, whilst still allowing you to customise it in line with your own communication style.

The competition project brief also includes a section dedicated to the URL of the results page, and the URL of the catalogue page. These allow you, right from the start, to create the results and catalogue pages however and wherever you wish – for example, on your Organisation’s website.

At the same time, however, we have decided to develop a catalogue creation service within the platform. We are still in the early stages; it will be based on the information the platform already holds: the entries accepted, section by section; the prize-winning entries, section by section and for the competition as a whole; with the option to add ‘extra pages’, for example to include images from the awards ceremony, information about the sponsoring federations or any sponsors supporting the competition. And if you’d like, you could also include a page thanking our platform for hosting you (at a reasonable cost).

So, for your competition, you will be provided with a link to start building the ‘version zero’ catalogue, to which you can then add and remove pages, choosing from various page types: title, text, image, image with caption, and others.

Finally: no, it’s not a substitute for Photoshop™, InDesign™ or Canva™. It does the job reasonably well and is included in the price.
