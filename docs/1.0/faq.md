## For Contest participants

### 1. Register

To register in platform only 3 elements are required: first name, last name and email.  
Obv, to partecipate at contest more personal details are required but there is a
separate form, voted to user contact details. Start from [here](https://yapcp.test/register)

### 2. Term of Service and Privacy Policy - GDPR

You can read our updated TOS [here](https://yapcp.test/terms-of-service) and
update Privacy Policy [here](https://yapcp.test/privacy-policy). As platform that
manage personal data from and for UE citizens, we are under GDPR regulation
and follow them. Because yaPCP is based in Italy,  Privacy Policy is written in Italian language.
