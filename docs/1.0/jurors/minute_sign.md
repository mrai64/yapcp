## Don't forget t sign the minutes

Once the jury’s work is done, and you’re exhausted after awarding the final prize and honourable mention, you’ll feel like saying thank you for the day, saying goodbye to everyone and heading home. 

But be careful: if you forget one thing, you’ll be haunted by it for days and weeks to come. Yes, there’s a signature missing from the jury’s minutes.

The platform allows you to access the contents of the minutes very quickly, almost immediately.
However, it is the organisers’ responsibility to prepare the minutes on the appropriate forms or on the required letterhead, so that you can sign them.  

The organisers have several ways of sending you the minutes. If the meeting is held in person, they will print the minutes on letterhead. If it is held remotely, they can send you a PDF of the minutes by email or via the conference system’s chat function. In any case, please remember that there is one last thing to do.
