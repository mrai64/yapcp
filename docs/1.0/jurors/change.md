## Vote review, individually

Once a round of voting has been completed, there may, on rare occasions, be a desire to review the votes cast in order to change them. Alternatively, you may realize that you have cast a vote unintentionally and wish to review it and, if necessary, correct it.

&nbsp;

Once you have left the voting panel, the competition dashboard displays a list of the sections and topics you are responsible for; by following that link, you can access the votes you have just cast. The list shows the most recent votes first.

&nbsp;

Is presented a group of six and every image had a link and a vote. After the vote change, image become first in row. How change vote is the same way to assign, and also in that panel an exit button put you out of page.
